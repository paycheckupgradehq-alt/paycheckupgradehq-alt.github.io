Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

Paycheck Upgrade is a personal-finance education brand. "Paycheck Upgrade Uploader" is an internal tool that we use to publish our own videos.

What the app does

The app signs in only to the Paycheck Upgrade YouTube channel and Instagram account, which we own, in order to upload and schedule our own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not ask other people to sign in.

Data we access

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and read basic channel information for our own channel (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • Instagram: permission to publish content to our own professional account.

We do not collect, sell, share or store personal data about viewers or any other users.

How credentials are stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a private file, and are never shared with third parties. They are used solely to upload our own content.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions and in Instagram's Settings → Website permissions.
